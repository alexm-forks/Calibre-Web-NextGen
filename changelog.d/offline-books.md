### Added

- **Download books to read without a connection.** A book's page in the new
  UI has a Download for offline button. Downloaded books appear in an "On
  this device" row at the top of the library and open in the reader with no
  connection, at the last place you reached on the device. The app also
  starts offline as the last signed-in user. Positions read offline are sent
  when the connection returns, ordered by when you read them. If the server's
  EPUB changes, the reader fetches the new file and replaces the downloaded
  copy, so positions stay aligned with the Kobo's copy.
