### Added

- **Swipe to turn pages in the reader.** Swipe left or right anywhere on the
  page. "Swipe to turn pages" under Reading appearance turns it off.
- **The reader's toolbar gets out of the way.** It floats over the page and
  hides when you turn a page by touch; tap the middle of the page to bring it
  back. Open panels keep it showing, and Tab brings it back for keyboard use.
- **"Follow device" page theme.** The reader switches between light and dark
  with your device. The classic reader shows the matching light or dark theme
  for this choice.
